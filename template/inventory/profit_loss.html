How profit is calculated

  • Gross profit = sales + shipping − cost of goods sold
  • Operating profit = gross profit − expenses ± stock adjustments
  • Net profit = operating profit + other income (rebates, services, interest…)
  • Margins are measured against sales revenue.
  • Cost of goods sold uses each product's weighted-average cost when it was sold (including shipping/customs on purchases).
  • Cancelled orders are excluded and their stock is returned.
